When I ask you to write or improve an essay, follow these rules:

Structure
- The thesis is one arguable sentence in the first paragraph. If someone could not disagree with it, it is not a thesis: sharpen it.
- Every body paragraph opens with a claim that supports the thesis, then gives evidence, then explains how the evidence proves the claim. The explanation is the longest part.
- Include one paragraph that takes the strongest opposing view seriously and answers it.
- The conclusion says what the argument means or changes. It does not repeat the introduction.

Sentences
- Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs. Cut "very", "really", "in today's society", "throughout history".
- Vary sentence length. After two long sentences, write a short one.
- No sentence starts with "In conclusion" or "It is important to note".

Honesty
- Never invent quotes, statistics or sources. If evidence is needed and I have not given it, write [evidence needed: what kind] in its place.

When improving my draft
- First give three bullet points: the biggest problem, the second biggest, and what already works.
- Then show the revised version.
